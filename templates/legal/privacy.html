Paid2Match (paid2match.work) is a social network for hiring referrals. This policy explains what we collect and what we do with it.

{{ _('What we collect') }}

  • Account information: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, GitHub, or Facebook, we receive your verified email address and name from that provider.
  • Profile information you choose to add: headline, bio, website and social links, avatar, skills, and location.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, polls, referrals (bounties), pitches, messages, endorsements, and reviews.
  • Trust and activity data: karma, trust score, verification tier, profile views, post views, and referral outcomes — the data the trust system runs on.
  • Payment information: payments and payouts run through Stripe (Stripe Checkout and Stripe Connect Express). We do not store your card numbers. Stripe handles that data under its own privacy policy.
  • Technical data: the cookies and local storage the site needs to work (see Cookie Policy), and basic server logs.

{{ _('What we use it for') }}

  • To run the service: accounts, feeds, spaces, referrals, and payouts.
  • To keep the community trustworthy: verification tiers, the pledge wall, response-time (SLA) tracking, moderation, and disputes.
  • To contact you about your account, a referral, or a payout. We do not sell your personal information.

{{ _('Your choices') }}

You can edit or delete your profile content, deactivate your account, or delete your account (self-service — confirm by typing DELETE; your data is anonymized on deletion).

If you are in Quebec, you can ask us for access to, or correction of, your personal information by writing to support@paid2match.work.

{{ _('Data deletion') }}

You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account (confirm by typing DELETE). Your personal data is anonymized on deletion.

You can also ask us to delete your data by emailing support@paid2match.work from the email address on your Paid2Match account.
